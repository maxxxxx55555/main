# How to Withdraw Funds (Russian Card Compatible)

## Important Legal Notice (Read First)

**Cryptocurrency mining and trading are NOT illegal in Russia**, but there are important rules:

1. **Mining is legal** — you are just earning RVN tokens using your own hardware
2. **Selling crypto for fiat (RUB) is legal** through registered exchanges
3. **Withdrawing to a Russian bank card IS possible** through authorized exchanges
4. **Taxes must be declared** if annual income exceeds 5 million RUB (simplified regime USN)
5. **Unregistered exchanges may block card withdrawals** — use only licensed/registered platforms

## Step-by-Step: From Mining to Your Russian Card

### Step 1: Mine RVN (Already Done)
Your miner earns RVN tokens. They go to your Ravencoin wallet.

### Step 2: Transfer RVN to an Exchange
Choose a platform that supports RVN deposits and RUB withdrawals:

**Recommended (RVN → RUB):**
- **Binance** — accepts RVN, supports RUB card deposits/withdrawals
- **Bybit** — accepts RVN, RUB card support
- **OKX** — accepts RVN, RUB support
- **XT.COM** — RVN trading pairs, RUB support

**Not recommended for Russian cards:**
- KuCoin (limited RUB support)
- Kraken (not available in Russia)
- LocalCoin, other P2P (higher risk)

### Step 3: Sell RVN for RUB
1. Go to the exchange
2. Find RVN/RUB trading pair
3. Sell your RVN for Russian Rubles
4. The RUB balance appears in your exchange account

### Step 4: Withdraw to Your Russian Card
1. Go to "Withdraw" → "Fiat" → "RUB"
2. Choose your bank card (Visa/Mastercard/Mir)
3. Enter amount (check minimum/maximum limits)
4. Confirm — funds arrive in 1-3 business days

**Important limits:**
- Binance: up to 2,000,000 RUB per day via card (for verified accounts)
- Bybit: up to 500,000 RUB per day
- OKX: varies by verification level
- Exchange fees: 0.5-2% for card withdrawals

### Step 5: Check Your Card
Open your banking app (Сбербанк, ВТБ, Альфа-Банк, Тинькофф, etc.) — the RUB should appear.

## What About Taxes?

**If you are an individual (not an entrepreneur):**
- Mining income is taxed at 13% (personal income tax)
- You must file a tax declaration if annual income exceeds 5 million RUB
- If under 5 million RUB — no declaration required (but keep records)

**If you register as an individual entrepreneur (ИП):**
- Can use simplified tax regime (УСН 6% or УСН 15%)
- Must file quarterly reports
- Requires registration with tax authority

**Recommendation:** For small-scale mining (under 5 million RUB/year), just keep records. For larger scale, consult a tax specialist.

## Risks and Warnings

1. **Exchange restrictions:** Some exchanges may limit card withdrawals. Always check current limits before depositing large amounts.

2. **Bank blocking:** Large regular crypto-related card transactions may trigger AML checks. Be prepared to explain the source of funds if asked.

3. **P2P alternatives:** If card withdrawals are blocked, use P2P trading on exchanges (sell RVN to another person, they send RUB to your card). This is legal but carries counterparty risk.

4. **Never use:** Unregistered exchanges, P2P without escrow, or direct transfers to unknown persons.
