# Can You Mine WITHOUT IP and WITHOUT Self-Employment?

## Short Answer: YES

**You can mine cryptocurrency as a regular individual (физлицо) without registering an IP (ИП) or self-employment (самозанятость).**

## Legal Basis

Under Russian law:
- Mining is NOT classified as entrepreneurial activity by default
- If you are not registered as IP or self-employed, you are a regular citizen (физлицо)
- Crypto mining on personal hardware is treated as personal property use, not business
- The key threshold: **if your annual income exceeds 5 million RUB, you must file a tax declaration (13% НДФЛ)**

## What This Means for You

### You CAN:
- Mine RVN on your personal GPU
- Keep RVN in your personal wallet
- Transfer RVN to an exchange
- Sell RVN for RUB
- Withdraw RUB to your personal bank card
- Do all of this as a regular citizen

### You CANNOT / MUST NOT:
- Claim mining as business activity without registration
- Exceed 5 million RUB annual income without filing taxes
- Use business deductions or expenses
- Issue receipts or invoices as a business
- Register as IP just to "be safe" (unnecessary)

## Tax Rules (Without IP)

### If annual income is UNDER 5 million RUB:
- **No tax declaration required**
- **No taxes to pay**
- Just keep records for yourself
- The exchange handles tax reporting automatically (they report to tax authorities)

### If annual income EXCEEDS 5 million RUB:
- File a tax declaration (Form 3-НДФЛ) by April 30 of the following year
- Pay 13% personal income tax on the excess
- You can deduct certain expenses (electricity, hardware depreciation)

## How Exchanges Handle Taxes

**Binance, Bybit, and other major exchanges automatically report to Russian tax authorities.** When you sell RVN for RUB and withdraw to your card:
1. The exchange records the transaction
2. They report it to the Federal Tax Service (ФНС)
3. You receive a tax notification if needed
4. You don't need to do anything — the exchange handles it

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to register as self-employed?**
A: No. Self-employment (самозанятость) is optional. It's only useful if you want to officially claim business expenses or work with clients who need receipts.

**Q: Do I need an IP?**
A: No. IP (ИП) is for business activities. Mining on personal hardware as a regular citizen is not business.

**Q: Will the bank freeze my card?**
A: Possibly, if you make large regular crypto-related withdrawals. This is an AML check, not a legal issue. Be prepared to explain the source of funds if asked. Using Binance (registered exchange) reduces this risk.

**Q: How much can I earn without taxes?**
A: Up to 5 million RUB per year without filing a declaration. After that, you must file and pay 13%.

**Q: What if I earn very little (like 1000 RUB per month)?**
A: No taxes, no declaration. Just keep the money. The exchange handles reporting.
